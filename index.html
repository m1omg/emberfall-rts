How to play

Desktop

  • Left drag — box-select units
  • Right click — move, attack, gather, build
  • WASD / edge / middle-drag — pan
  • Wheel — zoom · Space — jump to base
  • Ctrl+1…9 set group · 1…9 recall
  • A attack-move · S stop · H hold
  • Tab cycle idle worker · Esc cancel

Touch

  • One-finger drag — box-select (toggle the ✋ button to pan instead)
  • Two-finger drag — pan the map, pinch — zoom
  • Tap — select; with units selected, tap ground or a foe to command
  • Drag on the minimap — fly the camera anywhere
  • Long-press a command button for its details

Winning

  • Gather gold from mines and lumber (or brimstone) from the wilds.
  • Raise supply — Farms for the Kingdom, Soul Wells for the Legion.
  • Destroy every enemy structure and worker to win.
